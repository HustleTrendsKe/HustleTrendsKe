Who can join

Influencer registration for Kenyan content creators

Hustle Trends KE is a Kenyan marketing agency that runs campaigns and influencer marketing for brands. This page is for creators in Kenya who want to join our influencer network and be considered when a brand needs voices for a campaign.

You can register accounts on any of these platforms:

  • Instagram
  • TikTok
  • X (Twitter)
  • Facebook
  • YouTube
  • Snapchat
  • LinkedIn
  • WhatsApp Status

Whether you share comedy, fashion, food, sports, music, business tips or everyday life, what matters is an engaged audience and a clear rate for your posts.

How it works

How to join the network and get brand collaborations

  1. Choose the platforms you want to monetize.
  2. Add your profile link, follower count, rate per post in KSh and phone number.
  3. Submit your details to the Hustle Trends KE team.
  4. When a campaign fits your audience, we contact you on the number you gave us.
FAQ

Influencer registration questions

Who can register?

Kenyan influencers and content creators with an audience on Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook, YouTube, Snapchat, LinkedIn or WhatsApp Status.

What details do I need?

For each platform: your profile link, number of followers, your rate per post in KSh and a phone number.

Can I add more than one platform?

Yes. Add as many as you like, and edit or remove any of them before you submit.

What happens after I submit?

Your details go to the Hustle Trends KE team. When a brand campaign fits your audience, we reach out on the phone number you provided.

Do I set my own rate?

Yes. You enter the amount you charge per post for each platform.
